Take a picture of any web page, at any size.

Spectra Capture saves a picture of the page you are on. You choose the shape. The picture goes to your Downloads folder.

Start the guide

01Quick start

  1. Open a normal web page. Its address starts with http:// or https:// — local development servers such as localhost and 127.0.0.1 work too.
  2. Click the Spectra Capture icon in the toolbar.
  3. On page 1, pick a preset or type a width and height.
  4. Press CAPTURE at the bottom. A banner may appear briefly. Leave it alone.
  5. Find the picture in your Downloads folder.

Any http:// or https:// page works, including local development servers (localhost, 127.0.0.1) and addresses on your local network. Browser pages such as chrome:// or about:, extension pages and file:// pages are refused.

02The four pages

The popup is split into four pages, so everything fits without scrolling. Use the tabs at the top — the selected tab is highlighted with a warm wash, and its page number is a vermilion seal. The capture button stays at the bottom on every page.

1 · Size
The preset list, the width and height, the swap button, and the aspect ratio with its viewfinder.
2 · Output
Visible, full page, area or element, pixel density, format, JPEG quality, options, and your saved sizes.
3 · Settings
Delay, dark mode, sticky elements, Save As, clipboard, annotation, save notifications and the picker timeout.
4 · Console
Every step and every problem, in plain words. The number on the tab counts warnings and errors.

03Size and pixel density

Width and height are in CSS pixels. That is the size the page is drawn at. Pixel density multiplies it. For example, 1440 × 900 at 2× saves a 2880 × 1800 picture. Your screen does not change.

Presets come in three groups. Display holds a single Monitor option that detects your screen's resolution when you open the popup and adds it at 1× density — pick it to capture at the size you actually see. Devices are phones, tablets and laptops. Resolutions use 1× density, so the picture size matches its name; 4K UHD saves 3840 × 2160 pixels. The usual density controls still work with any preset.

Densities that are too large for your size are greyed out in the list. The hint under the list gives the highest one that works. In Full page mode the estimate assumes a tall page, so combinations that would certainly blow the pixel budget are greyed out too.

04Aspect ratio and the swap button

Pick a ratio such as 16:9. Then typing a width changes the height, and typing a height changes the width. The frame on page 1 shows the shape.

The swap button turns a portrait size into a landscape one, and the other way round.

05Full page and limits

Full page captures the whole height of the page. Very tall pages are captured in slices and stitched together locally, so height is no longer the limit it once was.

Pictures above 40 million pixels ask you to confirm first. The limit is 100 million pixels in total; these limits are not final. The Output receipt shows an estimate before you start.

06Area and element capture

Choose Area on page 2, then press CAPTURE. The popup closes and a dimmed overlay appears on the page. Drag a rectangle over the part you want; it is captured at your chosen density. Press Escape to cancel.

Choose Element to snap to one thing on the page — a button, image, card or section. Move the pointer and a coloured box follows the element under it; click to capture. The overlay is removed before the picture is taken, so it never appears in the image.

Nothing is sent anywhere. Selection uses an overlay drawn on the page, and the page is never changed by the extension.

07Settings

Page 3 holds the finishing touches. Delay waits 3, 5 or 10 seconds before capture, so you can open a menu or hover state. Dark mode for capture asks the site for its dark colour scheme. Hide sticky elements removes repeating headers and footers from full-page pictures.

Ask where to save shows the browser's Save As dialog. Copy to clipboard opens a small page that copies the picture after saving. Annotate before saving opens a drawing editor in a new tab, where you can add arrows, boxes, highlights and blur before saving the result.

Save notifications shows a system notification when a screenshot finishes saving. It is on by default; turn it off here if you would rather not see it. The picture still saves either way.

Motion effects on page 2 also controls the Area and Element pickers. While you pick, the corner brackets pulse together and a small accent square travels around the whole selection — two continuous, lightweight loops. Both overlays fade in and out, and the Element highlight glides softly when a bigger element changes to a smaller one (or back). Turn Motion off for a still, instant highlight. The overlay always uses the same light or dark colours as this popup, including its hard offset shadow.

Picker timeout is off by default, so an Area or Element pick waits for you instead of expiring after 60 seconds. Turn it on if you would rather have it give up automatically; press Escape at any time to cancel.

08Console

Page 4 lists each step and any problem in plain words. Nothing is shown as a pop-up message. Use Copy to copy the list, or Clear to empty it.

09Themes and motion

The switch at the top changes between light and dark. The popup and this guide remember the choice.

Motion effects can be turned off in the popup's Output page under Options. They also turn off when the computer asks for reduced motion.

10The debugger banner and privacy

Chromium shows a banner while a capture runs. It says that this extension started debugging the browser. The browser shows it for your safety, and it cannot be hidden.

Spectra Capture works on your computer only. It does not send your pictures, your pages, or your settings anywhere.

11Browsers

Spectra Capture is built for Chromium browsers version 116 or newer: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera and Helium. It uses the browser's debugger API to set an exact virtual viewport and capture at any pixel density.

Open a normal web page first — browser pages such as the New Tab page cannot be captured. Your browser's "ask where to save" setting is respected, and a system notification confirms each save unless you switch it off on the Settings tab. Spectra Capture works on your computer only: no network calls, no remote code, and no page content in logs.
